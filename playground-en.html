This portfolio

I wanted to understand what changes in the design process when you can build what you design directly. I used Claude Code CLI to go from mental wireframe to live site with no intermediaries: no handoff, no developers in between. The result is this portfolio: every visual decision was mine, every iteration happened in real time, with an AI agent as an execution partner.

Bollibelli

A stamp calculator based on the updated Poste Italiane rates, built with Lovable. It came from a real need: I do Postcrossing and penpalling, and I needed a way to figure out the exact postage based on destination country, weight, and the stamp combinations I already have at home. I made it free and shared it with the community.
